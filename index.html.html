Rung / Court Piece Rules

Objective: Two teams (South+North vs East+West) compete to win tricks. First team to 7 tricks wins the round. Winning 13-0 is called a Kot (Court)!
Dealing & Trump Selection: 5 cards are dealt first. The Trump-caller (player to dealer's left) chooses the Trump (Rung) suit based on those 5 cards.

✨ Blind Rung Option: If the caller cannot decide from 5 cards, they can choose 7th Card Rung or Last Card Rung. The suit of that card automatically becomes the Trump!

Trick Play: Players MUST follow suit if possible. If you don't have the led suit, you can play a Trump or throw off any other card. Highest card of led suit wins unless a Trump is played.
Single Sar vs Double Sar Variant:
  • Single Sar: Trick winner takes the trick immediately.
  • Double Sar: Tricks stay in center pool until a player wins TWO CONSECUTIVE TRICKS to collect the pool!
